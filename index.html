Frequently asked questions

Before you choose a course

Which courses are available?

The catalogue contains six AutoCAD courses (A1–A6), three Revit courses (RA1–RA3) and a complete library offer.

How are lessons delivered?

The proposal specifies on-demand video lessons inside a student login dashboard. The production video platform and access rules must be confirmed.

How does course access begin after payment?

The planned production flow grants course access only after server-side payment verification. A live payment gateway and enrolment service are not part of this static demo.

Are certificates, downloads and access periods included?

These details need confirmation for each course before publication. The site should show only approved course information.

Can I purchase a complete collection?

The complete library combines all six AutoCAD and three Revit courses. Current bundle price and purchase terms must be approved by the Academy.

How do I contact the Academy?

Use the enquiry section below to request course information. Add the Academy’s confirmed email, phone and messaging links before production launch.
