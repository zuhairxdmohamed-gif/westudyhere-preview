Frequently asked questions

Before you choose a course

How are lessons delivered?

The Academy's course format is recorded video lessons. Confirm access duration and available materials with the Academy before enrolment.

Which software courses are available?

The current collections are AutoCAD (A1–A6) and Revit (RA1–RA3).

Can I purchase a complete collection?

The complete library combines the six AutoCAD and three Revit courses. Contact the Academy for current purchase arrangements.

How do I access my courses?

Student portal access and payment-linked enrolment need to be activated on the production platform. Contact the Academy for current access instructions.
